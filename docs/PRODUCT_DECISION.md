# YESIF — product decision

Decision date: November 14, 2026. **GO for a bounded prototype; NO-GO for a validated-business claim or automatically replacing ShiftMargin.** Competition rules are supplied by the captain; the official FAQ was not attached and has not been independently checked.

## One user, one moment

US marketing/project coordinators aged 25–45 who personally want help responding to a new urgent request and can discuss priorities with their manager. The individual is the proposed buyer. Primary track: **Skills — evaluating workload and negotiating realistic priorities**.

Promise: **Know what has to change before you say yes.** Keep YESIF / Every yes needs an if. Brand availability and trademarks remain UNKNOWN.

The original overload calculator plus message is insufficient. Narrow to an inspectable, task-level negotiation: compare feasible deferrals against a smaller deliverable, understand each deadline, interactively combine changes with live recomputation, select a condition, edit the proposal, practice responding to pushback. No general planner or AI conversation platform.

## Evidence audit

- VERIFIED: [APA's 2023 US Work in America survey](https://www.apa.org/pubs/reports/work-in-america/2023-workplace-health-well-being) reports 77% experienced work-related stress in the previous month. Broad, older contextual evidence, not demand for YESIF or evidence about this exact age/job niche.
- VERIFIED: [Microsoft's April 23, 2025 report](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2025/04/23/the-2025-annual-work-trend-index-the-frontier-firm-is-born/) reports 80% of its global workforce sample lack time or energy. Not US-only and not a 2026 demand statistic.
- VERIFIED: [HSE demands guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/stress/standards/demands.htm) emphasizes achievable demands within agreed hours. UK guidance informs the mechanism; it is not US market validation or legal advice.
- INDICATED: paid adjacent planning and rehearsal products suggest some consumers pay for professional support. This does not establish sales volumes or willingness to pay for this product.
- HYPOTHESIS: a coordinator facing several such requests per month values a five-minute, private, specific proposal over a spreadsheet or generic prompt.
- UNKNOWN: acquisition cost, frequency, conversion, retention, improved negotiation outcomes, employer approval, and personal willingness to pay. No interviews, customers, payments, or usage claimed. The supplied Gallup 2026 percentage is omitted because it was not independently verified.

## Gates and choice

1. **Market: conditional pass for experiment.** Meaningful contextual problem; precise buyer and payment motivation hypothesis, but B2C payment evidence weak. Employer control limits usefulness.
2. **Competition: conditional pass.** Demonstrate named, fixed-safe alternatives and every deadline rather than just overload. No proven durable advantage. Reject the generic calculator and broad AI rehearsal alternatives.
3. **Execution: pass in design; verify at release.** React/strict TypeScript/Vite, pure quarter-hour engine, eight existing tasks, no API/account/backend. Plain CSS keeps this small; Tailwind adds no necessary capability here.

## Commercial choice

Free: unlimited calculations, alternatives, editable/copyable proposal, one explicit saved decision and practice. Reject weekly quotas.

Test a **$4.99 one-time Negotiation Kit**: multiple saved decisions, reusable scenarios, outcome notes, local export. Build the features behind a clearly labeled no-payment demo unlock. No checkout, transaction, subscription, or enforced entitlement claim. Browser-only storage is not secure paid access control.

Why not $6.99/month: recurring frequency and durable value are unproven; adjacent subscriptions offer much more. One-time pricing better matches episodic use, but is still HYPOTHESIS. Returning value is reusing known obligations and reviewing what was agreed when the next request arrives. Saving alone is a weak purchase reason.

Acquisition hypotheses: useful search pages for workload conversations, coordinator communities, independent career educators. No spam or outreach performed. Variable API cost is zero; hosting, payment processing if added, support and acquisition are not zero. At $4.99 gross revenue, even $5 CAC loses money before costs; use organic validation before paid acquisition. LTV and CAC UNKNOWN. Do not claim a plausible $100M ARR path for this narrow one-time utility. Expansion into recurring professional negotiation education would require separate demand and outcomes validation.

Next validation (not performed): observe five target users entering their own situation; check comprehension and usefulness without coaching; offer the actual kit at a real price; follow up on the next request. Stop or redesign if users cannot negotiate changes or free substitutes suffice. Product completion is not demand validation.
