# YESIF — Initial Product Hypothesis

Problem: Working adults struggle to evaluate new commitments and negotiate priorities when their workload is full.

Target: US professionals aged 25–45 with multiple deadlines and some ability to negotiate priorities.

Proposed value: Show the workload conflict, model feasible trade-offs, and prepare a concrete proposal for discussion.

Core mechanic: Capacity + deadline feasibility + interactive trade-off selection + editable proposal.

Differentiation: Go beyond overload warnings to explain exactly what must change.

Risks: Existing competitors; uncertain willingness to pay; uncertain repeat use; no proof of unique functionality.

These are hypotheses. Research and challenge them before implementation.
