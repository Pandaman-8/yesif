# YESIF V2 competition handoff — no deck

## Verified product story

Audience hypothesis: individual US marketing/project coordinators aged 25–45 facing a new request and able to discuss priorities with their manager. Primary track: Skills — evaluating commitments and negotiating realistic conditions.

**Every yes needs an if.** Interactive, inspectable trade-off decisions tied to real deadline feasibility, a concrete day-by-day allocation and a human proposal. This is a differentiation hypothesis, not a claim to be first or unique. Workminute already offers commitment checks, risk adjustment, response templates and separate task-level planning. Its planned Pro offer is not verified payment evidence. See PRODUCT_DECISION.md for the previously checked primary sources; V2 is not a new market-research validation.

## Live demonstration

1. Click **Try a real example**. The context is introduced without registration or a form: 13h committed + 5h requested against 15h available.
2. Read the first Friday conflict: 18h due, 15h available, 3h short.
3. Compare **Propose moving Weekly report** (13/15h) and **Make the new request a 2h deliverable** (15/15h). Each option is computed.
4. Expand **How your week could work** for the deferral: Mon QA 3h; Tue QA 2h + updates 1h; Wed updates 2h + presentation 1h; Thu presentation 3h; Fri presentation 1h with 2h unassigned. The report remains a 5h obligation awaiting a new agreed date, with future capacity unchecked.
5. Optional **Explore your own trade-off**: toggle the report or reduce scope; every change recalculates. Fixed tasks stay protected. An invalid choice cannot become a feasible proposal.
6. Choose an option; review/edit the short message. The detailed explanation and exact allocation remain inspectable. User edits are not automatically verified.
7. Practice a response to pushback tied to this request, actual Friday deadline and chosen condition. Inspect the four-part rubric; this is not an ability assessment.
8. Save a decision; record a self-reported outcome. Unlock the no-payment Kit demo to save more, export, and prepare a follow-up. No message is sent.

Designed for a useful result within five minutes; no timed target-user study has confirmed that claim. A demo can reach a calculated proposal in two clicks plus reading/scrolling.

## Independent judge inputs

- Early deadline: 3h/day; fixed client review 7h Tuesday; new client brief 1h Friday. Total 8/15h but Tuesday is 1h short. No false green plan. Editable renegotiation request names the conflict.
- Zero availability: positive request, all days zero. No complete allocation or feasible-proposal badge; a local draft can retain the unresolved request.
- Combined: 11h fixed + 2h negotiable; 6h new; 15h capacity. Scope-only 2h is one option; defer 2h and offer a 4h request is another computed option. Full-request deferral alone does not fit.
- Reserve: 1h reserved on Monday reduces effective reference capacity from 15h to 14h. Every calculation and daily allocation uses the reduced capacity.
- Custom scope description: enter what the smaller deliverable includes and show the exact text in the proposal.
- Data safety: type custom inputs, Reset example, Cancel; then Save draft and continue. Reload and resume the separate local draft. Empty numeric fields remain invalid.

## Free and proposed Kit

Free: unlimited real calculations, alternatives, daily allocation, simulation, editable/copyable proposals, contextual practice, one saved decision, outcomes/notes/reuse, a separate explicit recovery draft.

Kit: proposed **$4.99 one time**, no-payment session demo. Multiple records (50-device-record cap), inspect saved conditions together, local JSON export, follow-up message based on saved conditions and self-reported outcome. No dedicated quantitative comparison view, payment collection or secure paywall. Previously saved records remain readable after the demo ends.

Return-use hypothesis: revisit commitments and recorded terms when another request arrives, then follow through after discussion. Purchase motivation, frequency, CAC, LTV, retention and willingness to pay remain UNKNOWN. Local history alone is weak paid value. Follow-up support improves utility but does not establish demand.
