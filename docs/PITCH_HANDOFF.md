# YESIF — pitch handoff (no deck created)

## Demonstrable claim

A US marketing/project coordinator gets another urgent request. YESIF lets them change specific negotiable commitments and the requested scope, watch every weekday deadline recalculate, and prepare a proposal only when their chosen plan fits. Primary track: Skills.

**Every yes needs an if.** Promise: know what has to change before you say yes.

## Five-minute live demonstration

1. 0:00 — Landing: click **Try a real example**. The simulator opens directly, without a form or account.
2. 0:15 — Observe 13h committed + 5h new work, 15h available, 3h short by Friday.
3. 0:30 — Check **Defer Weekly report**: current-week work becomes 13h; inspect all five deadline checks. Fixed tasks cannot be toggled.
4. 0:50 — Reset; move the new-request slider to 2h: 15h/15h fits. Explain that a smaller deliverable needs agreement. Combine changes if useful.
5. 1:15 — **Edit workload**; use custom task names, hours, deadlines and negotiability. Recalculate. It is the same engine as the example.
6. 2:15 — Choose a feasible plan; edit/copy the proposal. It names the conditions and never claims a manager agreed or next week is free.
7. 3:00 — Try the manager pushback exercise; inspect its explicit four-part rubric. Feedback is fixed, not AI.
8. 3:40 — Save the decision; open **My decisions**, record an outcome, reuse the workload. Unlock the kit demo and save another scenario or export the notebook.

## Scenarios judges can reproduce

- Reference: 3h each weekday; QA 5h Wed fixed, customer updates 3h Thu fixed, report 5h Fri negotiable; new presentation 5h Fri. Full 18/15; defer report → 13/15; reduce request to 2h → 15/15.
- Early deadline: 3h each weekday; existing fixed work 7h Tuesday; new work 1h Friday. Total 8/15 fits weekly, but Tuesday 7/6 fails. No available deferral or new-scope reduction repairs the existing fixed conflict.
- Fits: 2h Monday, other days 0; no existing tasks; new brief 1h Friday. Accept without changes.
- Combined: 15h capacity; existing negotiable 2h and fixed 11h Friday; new 6h Friday. Defer 2h AND reduce new request to 4h → 15/15. A full-request deferral alone fails.
- Impossible: zero available capacity and positive request; no fake solution.
- Invalid: empty task name, negative hours, 0.1h or unavailable weekday; form errors block calculation.

## Free / proposed paid value

Free: unlimited analysis/simulation/proposals/practice, one saved-decision slot with notes and reuse. Kit: proposed **$4.99 one time**, multiple decisions (50-device-record guard), local JSON export, no-payment demo unlock for the visit. No checkout or transaction. Saved records remain accessible without re-unlocking. No quotas on the core product.

Retention hypothesis: new requests arrive, assumptions change, prior agreed priorities matter. This has not been observed in actual users. Saving alone is a weak moat and a weak payment argument.

## Evidence and commercial assumptions

See [PRODUCT_DECISION.md](PRODUCT_DECISION.md) for dated primary links and VERIFIED / INDICATED / HYPOTHESIS / UNKNOWN classifications. APA stress and Microsoft's global time/energy figures establish broad context only; omit unsupported Gallup 2026 claims. Workminute has both Commitment Check and task-level Smart Work Planner. Its advertised Pro is planned, not verified payments. Do not claim no direct competitors.

Differentiation hypothesis: user-controlled, combined commitment trade-offs with per-deadline recomputation and an immediately grounded proposal. Not visual polish, AI, email generation, or task-level planning alone. No durable moat established. No evidence this is better than an expert spreadsheet or every competing workflow.

Buyer: individual US coordinator aged 25–45, with authority to discuss priorities. B2C willingness to pay UNKNOWN; employer may be more natural buyer. One-time price is preferred over a subscription until frequency/value is demonstrated. Organic educational/search/community acquisition is a hypothesis; no outreach performed. At $4.99 gross, $5 acquisition cost is already uneconomic before other costs. CAC/LTV UNKNOWN. No credible $100M ARR claim for this narrow utility; expansion requires separate validation.

## Claims not to make

- “Validated demand,” “paying customers,” “proven retention” or invented conversion/revenue.
- “No competitors,” “first ever,” “guaranteed deadlines,” “manager approved,” “next week is available.”
- Treats/prevents burnout, employment-law protection, clinically validated skill improvement.
- AI-powered analysis, live payment, or subscription revenue.
- Browser/screenshot/production checks not explicitly recorded in QA_REPORT.md.
