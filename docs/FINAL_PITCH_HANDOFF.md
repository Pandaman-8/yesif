## Free, Kit and unvalidated claims

**Free:** unlimited checks and simulations, current-plan comparisons, editable/copyable proposals, two-step practice and one saved decision, with an outcome note and reuse. **Kit demo:** up to 50 local decisions, saved-snapshot comparison, Playbook, follow-ups and copyable/printable Brief. Existing records remain readable/exportable after demo access ends. Export is not an exclusive payment benefit.

The demo is free for the visit. **$4.99 one time is only a proposed price**, with no checkout or payment collection. Audience, acquisition, willingness to pay, return frequency, retention, differentiation and skill improvement are hypotheses. There is no validated moat, traction or commercial scale claim. The current test suite establishes implementation behavior, not product-market fit.

**Differentiation hypothesis:** interactive commitment trade-off simulation—choose specific negotiable obligations and scope, recalculate every weekday constraint, then carry the selected conditions into a concrete proposal and rehearsal. No claim of absolute uniqueness, superior demand, or ownership of this market. Task lists, attractive UI, workload totals and generated letters alone are not differentiators. ## Questions to prepare for

- **Why Skills?** It supports discussing priorities, scope and conditions, with authored two-step practice. Learning impact remains unmeasured.
- **Why switch from Workminute or a spreadsheet?** Demonstrate the selected obligation changes and per-deadline recalculation. Switching value is still a hypothesis to test.
- **What if the manager refuses?** A proposed change is conditional; revise inputs and recalculate. No approval is inferred.
- **Why pay?** Repeat-use tools might help preserve context; test the assumption before charging. No validated conversion or unit economics.
- **What are the limits?** One person, five weekdays, eight existing commitments, divisible work available from the start, entered estimates, no dependencies or verified later-week capacity. No guarantee of delivery or acceptable smaller scope.
- **Where is the data?** In this browser, with explicit saves/export. No account, cross-device sync or backend. Clearing browser data can remove records.
