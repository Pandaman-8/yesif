# V2.3 pitch handoff

## Verified capabilities to demonstrate

YESIF compares explicit commitment changes, checks every weekday deadline and prepares a conditional proposal. The shared visual week maps a real EDF allocation, with assigned, unassigned and reserved hours distinguished. The causal layer separates existing conflicts from pressure added by the request.

## Reproducible demonstration

1. Open the public product and choose **Explore an example**. Full workload is 18h / 15h.
2. Expand **Your week, mapped** on both alternatives. Moving Weekly report gives 13h / 15h and 2h unassigned; reducing the presentation to 2h gives 15h / 15h. The schedules differ. The report remains an obligation if deferred.
3. Open the optional simulator. Propose deferring Weekly report: the actual engine recalculates. Choose the plan; the proposal asks for agreement and warns that future capacity is unchecked.
4. Edit the workload: 3h per weekday, one Fixed Weekly report requiring 7h by Tuesday, and a 1h presentation by Friday. The week totals 8h / 15h, but Tuesday has 7h due / 6h usable.
5. YESIF explains that the earlier conflict already existed. It does not offer a Friday scope cut as a solution. Prepare the renegotiation request: it discusses the report and time before Tuesday. Expand the separate calculation evidence.
6. Optional: reserve 1h on Monday in the reference deferral scenario. Show 13h assigned / 14h usable, 1h unassigned and 1h reserved distinctly.

## Claims to avoid

- Do not say demand, willingness to pay, real Workminute Pro payments or business value are proven.
- Do not claim guaranteed delivery, manager approval, checked next-week capacity, AI coaching, automatic scheduling into a calendar, dependencies or actual deadline transfers.
- Do not call negotiation directions verified solutions.
- Do not claim a human usability study, full accessibility certification or native keyboard audit was completed for V2.3.
- Screenshots demonstrate layout, not business value.

The English PDF Pitch Deck is still a separate competition deliverable. It was explicitly excluded from this implementation task. Confirm the deck and submission requirements independently before submission; this release does not establish that a complete competition entry has been submitted.
